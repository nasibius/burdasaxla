# 28 May pilot: request for independent field assessment

**Purpose:** establish a trustworthy curb and access inventory before proposing new
pickup/drop-off allocations. AYNA is a potential stakeholder; no contract, approval
or mandatory integration is assumed.

**Boundary:** latitude 40.3725–40.3870, longitude 49.8390–49.8605. This is the current
pilot rectangle, not all of Baku. Map data: OpenStreetMap (ODbL), snapshot
2026-11-12T16:37:27Z. Basemap attribution: Esri and OSM contributors.

Three supported findings:

1. The supplied verified official-zone and field-observation inventory is empty;
   infrastructure shortage and walking coverage therefore cannot be established.
2. The current dataset contains 710 geographic access proxies. These identify places
   to investigate, not passenger volumes or confirmed coverage gaps.
3. A deterministic sample of 24 approximate unverified curb segments provides a
   manageable first survey batch. It excludes current prohibited segments and spaces
   survey targets; it is not an engineering suitability ranking.

**Requested field work:** inspect a small subset of the sample with an independent
reviewer. Record candidate/OSM ID, road side, measured curb length/width, signs and
markings, vehicle approach, competing uses, sidewalk connection, crossing and curb-ramp
access, source/photo reference, observer and date. Keep the camera position separate
from the feature position. Use the existing evidence/admin pipeline for traffic signs
and verified observations. Record `confirmed`, `rejected` or `needs data`, with reasons;
confirmation of an observation is not authorization of a new official stop.

Review the supply inventory's spatial completeness and freshness independently.
Validate pedestrian graph attachments and crossings before enabling served/gap
analysis. If a proposed allocation survives physical and legal review, the authorized
body must approve it through its own process; import the resulting validated official
record through the existing inventory workflow. A planning shortlist cannot do this.

Passenger movement counts, if supplied, must be authorized aggregate observations with
explicit periods, methods, licensing and publication permission. Define any future
outcome metric and baseline before the pilot; do not claim reductions in fines,
accidents or travel time without measurements.
